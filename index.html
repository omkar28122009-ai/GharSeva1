HELPFUL TO KNOW

Questions before you book?

Clear answers to common service-booking questions.

How do I book a service?

Open Services, choose a service and review its details. Add it to your booking, then enter your contact details, preferred visit time and service address before confirming.

Can I choose more than one service?

Yes. Select multiple services from the catalogue. Your booking page will list the selected services and combine their prices in the estimate.

What does the estimated total include?

The estimate shows the selected service subtotal, visit and platform fee, any valid applied discount, and the resulting estimated total. Additional work should be discussed and approved before it proceeds.

Can I track an existing booking?

Sign in and open My Bookings to review the current status, scheduled visit details, address and saved billing summary.
